Supplement

Creatine

Neural health, muscle recovery, and far less downtime between sets.

Read moreShow less

Great for general well being including neural health and muscle recovery. Creatine allows more water to be held in the muscles — during high intensity exercise your muscles readily absorb this and allow you to continue exercising with very little downtime between sets.

On the hair loss question: there is some small research stating creatine can cause hair loss, but this affects less than 1% of the population. Note that creatine is naturally occurring in steak, so chances are you'll be ok if you've ever eaten a cheeseburger — and in those extremely rare cases, simply not taking it reverts the changes.
